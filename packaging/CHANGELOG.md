# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release. Tested against PEAK 2.4.b.

- A live minimap, drawn by the game itself, on a handheld GPS in the corner of
  the screen.
- Markers for unopened chests, campfires, statues, belltowers, capybaras, mobs
  and the scoutmaster, each shown as a picture of itself. The pictures are
  photographed from your own copy of the game the first time you see one, so
  none of PEAK's artwork ships with the mod.
- Markers grow and lighten above you, shrink and darken below.
- Other climbers marked at any distance.
- Altitude and the distance to the nearest unopened chest.
- Zoom in fixed steps from 20 m to 2000 m across; three viewing angles.
- Client side: nobody else in the lobby needs it.
